Things I have built, from industrial control software running on a soft PLC to a real time simulation of evolution.

  • Luno

    • Flutter
    • Dart
    • SQLite

    A moon tracking app for planning night photography, still in development. Pick a place, then drag through time to see where the moon will be, how much of it will be lit, and what the sky will look like around it. All the astronomy is calculated on the device, in a background isolate, and cached in SQLite.

  • Evosim

    • Unity
    • C#
    • Genetic algorithms
    • JSON

    A three-dimensional, real time artificial life simulator. Creatures built from jointed physics blocks swim, eat and mate in a closed energy system. Each child's genes are crossed over from its two parents and then mutated, so the population evolves by natural selection.

  • ALT-1 Assembly & Leak Test Machine

    • IEC 61131-3
    • Ladder
    • Structured Text
    • OpenPLC
    • Modbus TCP
    • Python

    A simulated special purpose machine that presses a cap onto a valve body and simulates leak testing the assembly. A ladder and Structured Text program on the OpenPLC soft PLC controls it over Modbus TCP, with a Python plant simulator standing in for the machine. It was checked against a 30-test factory acceptance test, using the simulator's fault injection to produce jams and stuck sensors.
